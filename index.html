About HandOverIQ Backend

HandOverIQ is an AI-powered tool that helps developers understand any software project quickly. Instead of spending days reading long files and complex setups, you can upload your project documents, enter your Project & Document IDs, and get clear, easy-to-read handover summaries powered by Google Gemini AI.

AI Document Summary Ready
